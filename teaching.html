Credentials

  • Master of Music, Melbourne Conservatorium

"My goal is to help students uncover their rich, resonant, and free voice, and to find joy in their singing."

With 12 years of experience as a freelance performer with Australia's flagship arts organizations, I bring professional rigor to every lesson. My studio is a haven for adult learners, choral singers, and emerging professionals who want to understand the how and why of their voice.

Whether you are an engineer, a scientist, or a stage performer, lessons are tailored to your anatomy and goals. My approach focuses on:

Vocal Health & Mechanics
  • Improve breathing mechanics and postural alignment
  • Expand range and stamina (sing longer, with less effort)
  • Release tension to reveal your natural resonance
Artistry & Repertoire
  • Explore Classical, Musical Theatre, or Choral repertoire
  • Refine diction and language (It, Ger, Fr, Eng)
  • Connect emotionally to the text
Goals & Development
  • Preparation for auditions, choirs, or personal milestones
  • Confidence building for the "shy singer"
  • Strategic mentorship for emerging artists
