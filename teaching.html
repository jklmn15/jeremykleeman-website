Credentials

  • Master of Music, Melbourne Conservatorium
  • Winner, Australian Int. Opera Award
  • Masterclass Leader (WAAPA, Griffith)

"My goal is to help students find their authentic, relaxed and open voice, supported by the air and the body."

With 12 years of experience as a freelance performer with Australia's flagship arts organizations, I understand the demands placed on the modern singer. My studio welcomes students ranging from tertiary level to emerging professionals, as well as enthusiastic avocational singers.

Lessons are tailored to your specific goals, covering Classical and Music Theatre techniques. My approach focuses on three core pillars:

Vocal Health & Technique
  • Improve breathing technique and postural alignment
  • Increase vocal range, stamina, and dynamic control
  • Mastering the "legato line"
Performance Craft
  • Acting coaching and character development
  • Language preparation (It, Ger, Fr, Eng, Sp)
  • Overcoming perfectionism & performance anxiety
Industry Mentorship
  • Preparation for conservatory auditions & competitions
  • Navigating the freelance career
  • Music technology guidance
