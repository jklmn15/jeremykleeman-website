Vocal Tuition

Technique • Interpretation • Mentorship

"My goal is to help students find their authentic, relaxed and open voice, supported by the air and the body, and delivered with dramatic and musical intent."

As a performer who has navigated the industry from studying at the Royal College of Music in London to leading roles with all major Australian opera companies, I understand the demands placed on the modern singer. I offer tailored tuition for students ranging from tertiary level to emerging professionals, as well as enthusiastic avocational singers.

Whether you are preparing for conservatory auditions, an upcoming role debut, or simply want to unlock the unique colour of your voice, I provide a supportive and professional environment to explore your potential.
